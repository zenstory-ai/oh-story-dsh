## MOTION-EP001-001 · 年轻的手

- 分镜：SHOT-EP001-001
- 时长：5s
- 起始帧：右手放松摊在玻璃桌面。
- 终点：手指微收，材料纸角碰到指尖。

### 可复制提示词
> Hold the camera completely still. The relaxed right fingers slowly curl by less than one centimeter as if testing whether the hand is real. During the final second, the corner of a paper stack slides in from frame right and gently touches the index fingertip. Preserve the young hand, glass reflection, desk, file-box background, daylight direction and shallow depth of field. No extra hands, no camera shake, no text.
